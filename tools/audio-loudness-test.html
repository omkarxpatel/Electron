Audio Visualizer — loudness diagnostic

Self-contained. Doesn't touch the app. Two tests below. Run both, paste the output back.

[A] Synthetic limiter A/B (instant)

Feeds a 1 kHz sine at four levels (0, −3, −6, −12 dBFS) through the app's current limiter and a relaxed candidate. Pure math via OfflineAudioContext. No audio capture, no permissions. Tells us how much loudness the current limiter is costing.

[B] AGC capture test (interactive)

Captures audio twice via getDisplayMedia. First call uses default constraints (likely AGC on); second uses explicit {autoGainControl:false, echoCancellation:false, noiseSuppression:false}. Each capture runs for 6 seconds. Reports captured peak + RMS.

  1. Open Spotify (web or app) and start playing a track at a steady level. Don't change anything between the two captures.
  2. Click Capture #1 (defaults). When the browser prompts to share, pick This Tab with "Also share tab audio" on, or pick Entire Screen with "Share system audio" on (the wording varies by Chromium version).
  3. Wait for the 6-second measurement to finish.
  4. Click Capture #2 (AGC off) and repeat with the same source.
  5. Compare the two rows.
